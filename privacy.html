Privacy Policy

Prince Revenge · effective 19 November 2026

In short

Prince Revenge does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your game stays on your device.

What the game stores, and where

Your progress (city, heroes, troops, items), the name you give your lord and your settings (language, music, sound) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game sends over the internet

Nothing. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline. We do not use third-party services that receive your data, and we do not sell or share any data.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you are in full control: clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes your saved game and settings completely. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds online play, purchases or anything else that involves your data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: write to the developer, HatchyVerse, at hatchyverse@gmail.com.
