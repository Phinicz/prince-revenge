Contact us

For help with the game, a bug report or a question, write to hatchyverse@gmail.com. Please tell us your phone model, the version of the game (Settings) and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your phone only. The game needs no account and no internet connection. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the saved game.

Does the game cost anything or have purchases? No. The game is free and has no in-app purchases.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the Privacy Policy.
